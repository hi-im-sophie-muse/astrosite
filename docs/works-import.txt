brain dormant & suspenselessly magpietyish “oh geez do not abbreviate this title i'm sorry” 
Date of Completion: February 2021 
Instrumentation: Piano, Kazoo, Slide Whistle, Voice (optional), post-perf. video editing. 
Comments: A piece with the requirement that I can play it. Wanting to perform is a rare occurrence, I generally dislike it very much. To cope with the cognitive dissonance of needing to perform and getting an anxiety attack every time I think about this piece, I fractured my character into three distinct people: the composer, the performer, and the editor. They all hate each other. They are all me.

Desert Sandbox 
Date of Completion: March 2019 
Instrumentation: Flute, Sitar, Percussion, Cello 
vertically structured composition for use in a modified version of the WWise Adventure Game 
Comments: Four tracks with (A) as relatively static, (B) as main melody, (C) as combat indication and (D) as constant drone. A4/B4 are intentionally empty to allow for thinning/swelling to naturally occur. Only one line from each track will play e.g. A1B2(C1)D1. Displayed during UC Santa Barbara’s Winter 2019 Art Walk.
