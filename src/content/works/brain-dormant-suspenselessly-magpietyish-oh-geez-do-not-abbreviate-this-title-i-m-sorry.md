A piece with the requirement that I can play it. Wanting to perform is a rare occurrence, I generally dislike it very much. To cope with the cognitive dissonance of needing to perform and getting an anxiety attack every time I think about this piece, I fractured my character into three distinct people: the composer,the performer,and the editor. They all hate each other. They are all me.
