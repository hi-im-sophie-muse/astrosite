Four tracks with (A) as relatively static,(B) as main melody,(C) as combat indication and (D) as constant drone. A4/B4 are intentionally empty to allow for thinning/swelling to naturally occur. Only one line from each track will play e.g. A1B2(C1)D1. Displayed during UC Santa Barbara’s Winter 2019 Art Walk.
