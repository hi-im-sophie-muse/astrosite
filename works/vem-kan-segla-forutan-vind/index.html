  1. Vem Kan Segla Förutan Vind? (2:45) — Multiple transformations of the primary theme in the aforementioned Swedish folk lullaby.

for piano

Title translates to “Who can sail without the wind?”. Section II is a portmanteau of Gretchen am spinnrade (F. Schubert) & “La valse d’Amélie” (Yann Tiersen)
