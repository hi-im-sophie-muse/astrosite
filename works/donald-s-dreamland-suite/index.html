Donald's Dreamland Suite

single · 2022

  • YouTube

tracks

  1. Donald's Dreamland Playground (0:33) — Track meant to calm someone enough to lull them to sleep if they were close to it.
  2. Donald's Dreamland Street (0:30) — Track intended to depict fleeting thoughts as one falls asleep, via dueted melodies.
  3. Donald's Dreamland Building (0:42) — Track that tries to capture the straighter feeling of the original building's rhythm, with just a pinch of western classical theory.

for piccolo, alto flute, clarinet in Bb, glockenspiel, harp, piano

Multimovement. Reskinning (re-musicking?) three of many tracks from Disney’s ToonTown Online.
