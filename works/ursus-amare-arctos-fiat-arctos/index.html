  1. Ursus Amare Arctos, Fiat Arctos! (0:21) — Care Bears try to care for an unhappy soul, only to be rebuffed.

for descant recorder, horn in F, tuba, bass drum, vocalist

inspired by a random screenshot of the show Care Bears (deleted). Title translates to Bear Love Bear, Let there be Bear! as a jab to scientific bear names and “fiat lux,” the University of California’s motto.
