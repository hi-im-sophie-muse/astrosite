  1. S.C.B (9:56) — vocalist finds bees everywhere in increasingly concerning places, accompanied by bees on keys. text from SCP bees entry.

for piano, vocalist

SCP is an online collaboration-fiction website that secures,contains,and protects society from entities that pose a threat to humankind’s physical or psychological wellbeing. This particular entity is bees. The text comes from a deluded man’s monologue about bees in hypothetical situations.
