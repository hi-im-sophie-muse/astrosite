tracks

  1. Brrrgh Playground (0:57) — Variant that plays when idling in the playground, featuring piano + glockenspiel + bassoon
  2. Brrrgh Street (1:01) — Variant that plays when not fighting cogs on the street, featuring piano + glockenspiel + cello
  3. Brrrgh Building (0:56) — Variant that plays when in a building, featuring piano + bassoon + cello

for piano, glockenspiel, bassoon, cello

Certain traits like “icy,” “cold,” etc elicit a fairly narrow, but not intolerable amount of stereotypically associated sounds. It was fun trying to figure out how to depict the Brrrgh in ways that weren’t already used in the original tracks.
