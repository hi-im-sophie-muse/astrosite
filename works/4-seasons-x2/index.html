4 Seasons (x2)

album · 2021

  • Full work + music video

tracks

  1. Autumn I (1:17) — Primary inspiration is the concept of leaves blowing around as the temperatures settle.
  2. Autumn II (0:55) — Halloween, the flipside of the daytime feeling of the other track plus.
  3. Winter I (1:00) — Icy, sharp, cold, perhaps even solemn and makes you feel all alone.
  4. Winter II (1:12) — Holiday, the positive side of being cold all the time. It's like stepping into a cabin after Winter I.
  5. Spring I (1:11) — Not supposed to pick favorites, but this is my favorite I think? Imagine sitting in a forest whose temperatures relax you and the creatures around you.
  6. Spring II (1:01) — Reprise of Autumn I, to depict the idea of leaves regrowing and signaling the cycle of new life.
  7. Summer I & II (2:03) — Both tracks merge into each other this time, and that's because its friends wanted to join in on the fun.

for piccolo, flute, alto flute, oboe, clarinet in Bb, trumpet in C, glockenspiel, xylophone, vibraphone, percussion, cymbals, sleigh bells, tambourine, synth, harp, ukulele, violin, viola, double bass, cello

Multi-movement work consisting of 2 iterations of each season starting with autumn. At an early point in my life, I was indirectly affiliated with the luxury hotel chain mentioned in the title but that company is in no way associated with this piece. Individual tracks can be listened to separately, the whole work includes transitions to smooth thigns out.
